# Principles

## Ideas deserve version control.

Ideas evolve. Their history matters as much as their final form.

---

## Markdown is the source of truth.

Ideas should remain portable, readable and future-proof.

---

## Conversations produce artifacts.

A conversation is valuable only if it leaves behind reusable knowledge.

---

## AI agents have roles.

Different agents specialize in different responsibilities.

---

## Human judgment is final.

AI assists. Humans decide.

---

## Everything starts as an experiment.

Hypotheses are more valuable than certainty.

---

## Preserve evolution.

Do not hide failed ideas.
They are part of the discovery process.

---

## Build in public whenever possible.

Open knowledge accelerates better ideas.
